How it works

From brief to delivery in three steps

The same flow works for both sides of the table, so nobody has to guess where things stand.

01

Post or build

Clients describe the project, budget and required skills. Freelancers build a profile with skills, experience and portfolio.

02

See the fit

Every application comes with a match prediction — strong, moderate or low — and the skills that drive it.

03

Hire and deliver

Clients shortlist and hire. Both sides track progress until the work is complete and rated.

Two AI capabilities

Machine learning for matching. Generative AI for writing.

Matching uses a Random Forest classifier trained on freelancer–project data. Writing help comes from a large language model that reads your profile and the project brief together.

Match predictionA clear label, a percentage and the evidence behind it — skills overlap, experience and relevant technologies.

Proposal generatorDraft a tailored proposal for a specific project, then edit, copy or save it before sending.

Profile summaryTurn your skills and highlights into a concise summary clients can scan in seconds.
